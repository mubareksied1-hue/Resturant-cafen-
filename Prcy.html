Privacy Policy & Terms

Last updated: August 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when you subscribe to our newsletter, reserve a table, or contact us. This may include your name, email address, phone number, and reservation details.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data helps us process your table bookings smoothly, send you updates about new menu items and special offers, and improve your overall dining experience at Cafen.

3. Cookies and Tracking

Our website uses basic cookies to enhance your browsing experience. These cookies help us understand how you interact with our pages so we can make layout improvements.

4. Terms of Service

By making a reservation at Cafen, you agree to arrive within 15 minutes of your chosen time block. Please contact us directly if you need to cancel or change your booking details.
